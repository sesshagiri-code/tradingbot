# AI/ML Trading Lab (FastAPI + React + Upstox)

Backtesting, ML signals and paper trading on Upstox market data. Paper trading is the default. A live-order endpoint exists but is disabled unless `LIVE_TRADING_ENABLED=true` and each request sets `confirm=true`.

## Risk

- **No proven edge.** Use the **Edge check** (`POST /api/backtest/edge-check`) to compare a rule-based strategy with buy-and-hold on the most recent out-of-sample window. A single window can be lucky. Nothing here has shown it beats buy-and-hold over a meaningful period.
- **Stops are polled, not streamed.** The stop monitor (`/api/paper/stop-monitor`) checks live prices every 15–900 seconds during market hours. A fast move can pass a stop between checks, and the exit then happens at the price seen, which can be below the stop.
- **ML is thin.** The outcome model gates buys only after 60+ completed trades across at least 5 symbols and 20 trading days.
- **Paper fills are optimistic, though less so.** Fills include adverse slippage (`PAPER_SLIPPAGE_PCT`, default 0.05%). Orders are capped at `PAPER_MAX_VOLUME_PCT` (default 10%) of the latest 5-minute volume, and the rest is left unfilled. Real orders still face gaps, queueing, and rejections that this does not model. Index instruments without volume are not capped.
- **Market risk.** Intraday trading in NSE equities loses money for most retail participants, and this setup does not change those odds.

## Upstox setup
1. Create an app at https://account.upstox.com/developer/apps; set redirect URI to `http://localhost:8000/api/auth/callback`.
2. Put the key/secret in `backend/.env`, click **Login with Upstox** in the UI (token is stored for the day).
3. Without credentials the app falls back to deterministic synthetic data so everything still works (results on synthetic data are not meaningful).

## Features
- **Market**: searchable NSE cash-equity universe from Upstox's daily instrument master, plus NIFTY 50 and BANK NIFTY. The master is cached locally and refreshed daily; symbols can also be force-refreshed from the header. Historical candles (1min / 5min / 30min / day) use Upstox v2 with chunked fetching and CSV cache; 5-minute bars are aggregated from complete 1-minute candles. Intraday lookback is subject to Upstox's per-interval history availability.
- **Backtest**: SMA crossover, RSI, ML (gradient boosting / random forest, walk-forward retraining), and a 5-minute breakout strategy. The **NIFTY 50 portfolio** mode runs daily long-only backtests for available constituents and aggregates the portfolio against an equal-weight buy-and-hold benchmark; initial capital is allocated equally per stock, and missing/failed symbols are reported with their allocation held in cash. It is available at `POST /api/backtest/nifty50`. Breakout rules require a close above the prior 20-bar resistance, a retest that holds within 3 bars, breakout volume above 1.2× its prior baseline, RSI at least 50, price above session VWAP, and walk-forward ML P(up) at least 0.55. The 5-minute direction model is scored on out-of-sample historical bars; paper buys also require validation AUC above 0.5. OI is used only when positive candle OI is available and otherwise reported as unavailable. Breakout entries risk at most 1% of equity by default, cap position value at 20%, use the retest low as a stop, and reject stop distances above 2%; thresholds are configurable. Backtests use next-bar-open execution, fees, slippage, and stop-loss; metrics include return, CAGR, Sharpe, max drawdown, win rate, profit factor, vs buy & hold. Closed trades from real-data non-breakout backtests can train the paper-trading outcome model; synthetic runs are excluded.
- **Indicators and models**: `GET /api/indicators` returns RSI(14), EMA(12/26), ATR(14), session VWAP, and prior-session CPR with OHLCV candles. Direction models support gradient boosting and random forest by default, with XGBoost available by selecting `"models":["xgboost"]` in `POST /api/train/auto` or `"model":"xgboost"` in prediction/training requests. Walk-forward predictions use chronological out-of-sample evaluation.
- **Best Stocks screener and auto paper trading**: the **Best Stocks** tab trains Random Forest and XGBoost direction classifiers per symbol using chronological walk-forward validation, then ranks NIFTY 50, Sensex, or core equities using a 50/50 blend of model consensus next-session up probability and daily EMA trend, 20-day momentum, RSI, volume confirmation, CPR, and ATR. Model probabilities and validation metrics are shown separately. The optional Best Stocks auto trader must be explicitly started in the UI; it uses Upstox data/quotes but submits simulated orders only to the SQLite paper account. Default entries require both models at 55%+ and a technical score of 50+, exits trigger if either model falls below 45%, with at most five open positions and a 20% per-symbol allocation cap. It scans during NSE market hours and uses the paper slippage and volume caps. Start/stop/status and a one-cycle paper-run API are available at `/api/paper/best-stocks/auto` and `/api/paper/best-stocks/run`. It requires Upstox login, skips symbols without usable real candles, and makes no promise of performance.
- **Options scanner and paper trading**: the **Options** tab analyzes the NIFTY 50 index, BANK NIFTY index, and NIFTY 50 constituent stock options using Upstox option chains and walk-forward Random Forest/XGBoost underlying signals. It defaults to scanning both indexes and refreshes recommendations every 60 seconds while the tab is open; users can pause refresh or select other universes. It considers near-ATM contracts with aligned model direction, validated AUC, positive volume/open interest, and bounded bid/ask spread. Index chains use the nearest weekly expiry (with monthly fallback); stock chains use the nearest monthly expiry. Manual paper orders allow Buy-one-lot and Sell-to-close only, validate current contract data, and limit premium cost to 2% of paper equity; live option orders and short options are not supported. Optional auto paper trading is off until explicitly started; it uses simulated orders only, buys at most one lot per underlying, caps each position's premium cost at 2% of paper equity, and allows at most three option positions. Positions exit at a 30% premium stop, 50% premium target, or at least one weekday before expiry. Auto paper trading checks every 60 minutes during NSE market hours; exits are polled, so a fast price move may pass the stop. API endpoints: `GET /api/options/recommendations`, `GET /api/paper/options/positions`, `POST /api/paper/options/order`, `GET/POST /api/paper/options/auto`, and `POST /api/paper/options/run`.
- **Reinforcement-learning research**: `backend/app/rl_envs.py` provides a discrete hold/long/flat PPO environment and a continuous 0–100% allocation SAC environment. Both simulate next-bar-open paper fills with fees, slippage, and volume caps; neither submits broker orders. Train with `train_ppo` / `train_sac` after installing optional dependencies using `pip install -r requirements-rl.txt`. These policies are research tools, not evidence of profitable strategies.
- **Paper trading**: virtual ₹10L account in SQLite, searchable manual buy/sell for any symbol in the NSE cash-equity master, risk limits (default 20% max per symbol and 5 open positions), and ML signals. Limits are enforced by the API for manual and automated buys. The dashboard reports fees, realized win rate, profit factor, expectancy, average/best/worst trades, today's realized P&L, and loss streak. The shared trade-outcome model learns across symbols from completed paper trades and real-data backtests. It requires at least 60 outcomes, including 20 profitable and 20 unprofitable trades, drawn from at least 5 symbols and 20 trading days, then checks a chronological out-of-sample segment before allowing buys. Validated outcome models are checkpointed in `backend/data/models` and reused until training examples change. **Run 5-minute breakout** uses real intraday candles and a walk-forward validated direction model, sizes paper entries to the configured per-trade risk, stores the retest-low stop, and checks that stop or a VWAP/RSI exit when the signal is run again; stops are not continuously monitored. OI is skipped if reliable data is unavailable. **Forward check (no orders)** records one real-data, next-daily-bar forecast per configured symbol; run it again after a later daily candle to score predictions. Use **Train from outcomes** to reconstruct eligible legacy paper trades and check readiness. Resetting the paper account clears balance, positions, and orders, but keeps saved model-training examples and forecast history. Real Upstox historical data is required for training and automated orders.

- **10-point support/resistance breakout (paper only, long only)**: resistance is the highest high of the prior 20 five-minute candles. A long is opened when the last closed candle closes at least 10 points above it, with a 10-point stop that trails the highest close upward only. Max 2 buys per day, and open positions are squared off from 15:15 IST. `POST /api/paper/points-breakout` runs one evaluation. `POST /api/paper/points-breakout/auto` with `{"enabled": true, "interval_seconds": 60}` runs it on a timer during NSE hours (09:15–15:30 IST, weekdays); `{"enabled": false}` stops it, and `GET` returns status. The stop-loss is checked only on each evaluation, not continuously. Support (prior 20-candle low) is reported for context only.
